HuaPass 華語通 — 隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 Effective date: 2026-07-11

中文

HuaPass 華語通（下稱「本應用程式」）由 Stronghold 開發，供準備 TOCFL 華語文能力測驗的學生使用。我們重視您的隱私：

  • 完全離線可用：本應用程式的所有學習功能（詞卡、測驗、聽力、閱讀、模擬考）均在您的裝置上執行，學習進度僅儲存在您的裝置中。
  • 不強制收集任何個人資料：使用本應用程式不需要註冊帳號。
  • 選擇性的進度同步：若您就讀的學校提供教師儀表板服務，您可以「自行選擇」在設定頁填入姓名、學號與學校伺服器網址，按下「儲存並同步」後，您的姓名、學號、詞彙學習進度與模擬考成績將傳送至該學校自行架設的伺服器，供您的老師追蹤備考進度。此資料傳送給您的學校，而非本應用程式開發者；資料的後續處理由該學校負責。您可隨時停止同步（清空伺服器欄位即可）。
  • 無廣告、無追蹤：本應用程式不含任何廣告、分析或第三方追蹤工具。
  • 兒童隱私：本應用程式不向任何使用者（包括兒童）強制收集個人資料。

English

HuaPass (the "App") is developed by Stronghold for students preparing for the TOCFL exam.

  • Fully offline: All study features run on your device. Your learning progress is stored locally on your device only.
  • No mandatory data collection: No account or registration is required to use the App.
  • Optional progress sync: If your school operates a teacher dashboard, you may voluntarily enter your name, student ID and your school's server address in Settings. When you tap "Save & sync", your name, student ID, vocabulary progress and mock-test scores are sent to that school-operated server so your teacher can follow your exam readiness. This data goes to your school — not to the App developer — and its further handling is the school's responsibility. You can stop syncing at any time by clearing the server field.
  • No ads, no tracking: The App contains no advertising, analytics or third-party trackers.
  • Children's privacy: The App does not require personal data from any user, including children.
